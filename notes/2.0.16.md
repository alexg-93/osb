# Party assist: follow the party leader's target

- New on the Party page: **Assist the party leader** (off by default). Party members attack the monster the party leader is hitting, for the monster classes with **Assist** ticked under Combat → Monster classes (Giant and Party giant by default).
- For the classes without the tick the party spreads out: each member goes first for the monsters it is nearest to, and leaves a monster a party member is already fighting, unless it attacks you or you hit it first.
- The leader is the game's party leader; when they are dead, gone, too far away (Leader counts within, 100 m by default) or ticked in the Protect party members list, the next living member of the party list leads. No chat message and no new network request: every bot works it out from what the game already shows.
- Use the same settings on every character of the party (the switch, the ticked classes and the Protect list), so they agree on the leader.
- Protect party members, monsters hitting you, potions, town trips, Skip these mobs and the hunting area still come first.
