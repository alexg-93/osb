# Lure works in caves

- **Lure instead of hunting** now works in caves. Before, the lure stood at the hunting spot with "Outdoor collision data required" while the rest of the party hunted.
- In a cave the lure walks in a straight line to each pull point and back, so pick pull points you can reach straight from the hunting spot. Outside caves nothing changes.
- When the pull points or the hunting spot were saved on another map, for example on another cave floor, the bot now stops and says so. Save them again where you are.
